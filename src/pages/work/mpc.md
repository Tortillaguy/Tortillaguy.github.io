I joined MPC (Moving Picture Company) in February 2018 as a VR engineer, on a team of fewer than ten. Most of my year there went into two projects: Spotify for Magic Leap, and an AR app for National Geographic.

## Spotify for Magic Leap

Spotify's audio came through a C++ plugin for Magic Leap's platform. I built the Unity app that wrapped that plugin and streamed music through it, and the interface you see around you in the room: album art pinned to the walls, the player and its controls floating in space.

I left MPC in February 2019. The app shipped in November 2019.

## National Geographic

The other project was an AR app for National Geographic that ran on phones and tablets. It placed a dinosaur on a real surface in front of you and worked as an interactive guide to its anatomy. You could explore the parts of the dinosaur, with audio cues and timelines explaining each one.
